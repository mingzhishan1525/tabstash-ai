# Chrome Store Launch Assets - TabStash AI

## Chrome Store Listing

### Name

TabStash AI

### Short Description

Close tabs without losing ideas. Stash pages, get AI summaries, and archive key points to Notion.

### Detailed Description

TabStash AI helps Notion users and knowledge workers clear browser clutter without losing useful ideas.

When you find a page worth keeping, stash it with one click. TabStash AI saves the selected page, closes the tab after it is safely stored, and turns the readable content into a compact AI summary with tags and key points. When you are ready, send the organized result to your own Notion database.

Built for research sessions, reading queues, product work, writing, learning, and anyone who ends the day with too many tabs open.

Key features:

- One-click tab stash for pages you choose to save
- AI-generated brief summaries
- Tags and key points for faster review
- Reopen saved pages when needed
- Archive selected content to your own Notion database
- Local Inbox persistence to reduce accidental data loss
- Batch stash current window tabs
- Designed for Notion users and knowledge workers

TabStash AI only processes pages you actively choose to stash. It is not a browser history tracker and does not automatically read pages you have not selected.
